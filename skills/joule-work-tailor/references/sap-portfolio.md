## Strategic Vision & Commercial Journeys

### The Autonomous Enterprise

SAP's vision for the future of business — where people set direction and AI executes, with governance at every step. Brings together AI engagement (Joule), intelligent business execution (SAP Autonomous Suite), and a governed AI foundation (SAP Business AI Platform). Official page: sap.com/products/autonomous-enterprise.html

| Official Name | Notes |
|---|---|
| Autonomous Enterprise | SAP’s end-state vision and destination for all customers; not a product name — always without 'SAP' prefix |
| SAP Autonomous Suite | The integrated SAP application suite connecting ERP, LoB, data, and AI processes end-to-end |

### Cloud ERP Transformation Journeys

Both journeys lead to the Autonomous Enterprise.

| Official Name | Acronym | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| RISE with SAP | RISE | Transformation journey for existing on-premises SAP customers moving to SAP Cloud ERP Private |
| SAP GROW | GROW | Cloud ERP journey for new SAP customers, built on SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition (SAP Cloud ERP); also referred to as “GROW with SAP” — both are officially used in SAP materials |

## Naming Rules for Generated Text

1. Always write **SAP BTP** (never just 'BTP') in customer-facing or profile text.
2. Always write **SAP HANA Cloud** — never 'HANA Cloud' (without the SAP prefix) or 'HANA' alone.
3. Always write **SAP Datasphere** (never 'Datasphere' alone or 'DWC').
4. Use **SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition** for the technical product name; use **SAP Cloud ERP** for the simplified commercial name in SAP GROW context.
5. Use **SAP S/4HANA Cloud Private Edition** for the technical product name; use **SAP Cloud ERP Private** for the commercial package in RISE with SAP context.
6. Use **Joule** without the ‘SAP’ prefix — that is the official brand style. Same for Joule Work, Joule Agents, Joule Assistants, Joule Studio.
7. Use **SAP GROW** as the primary name; “GROW with SAP” is acceptable — both are officially used in current SAP materials.
8. Use **SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC)** on first mention; BDC thereafter.
9. Use **SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)** on first mention; SAC thereafter.
10. Use **SAP Business AI Platform (BAIP)** when positioning the umbrella; use **SAP BTP** when referring to the platform product specifically.
11. Use **AI Foundation** (no ‘SAP’ prefix) when referring to the developer toolkit on BTP.
12. **LeanIX** alone is acceptable after first mention of ‘SAP LeanIX’.
13. **Signavio** alone is acceptable after first mention of ‘SAP Signavio’.
14. Use **Joule Agents** and **Joule Assistants** as distinct names — do not conflate them with each other or with ‘Joule’ generically. Joule Agents execute tasks autonomously; Joule Assistants are role-based conversational partners.
15. Use **Autonomous Enterprise** (no ‘SAP’ prefix) for the vision; use **SAP Autonomous Suite** for the integrated product suite.
16. **SAP Databricks** is the official product name; “SAP Databricks in Business Data Cloud” is the full contextual label used on sap.com.
17. Use **SAP WalkMe** on first mention; “WalkMe” alone is acceptable after introducing “SAP WalkMe”.

## SAP Internal Role Taxonomy (2026)

Use these official titles in generated Role Description text.

### Solution Advisory (Presales)
All presales roles carry the **Solution Advisor** title. Sub-teams align to portfolio areas:
- **Solution Advisor — BAIP** (Business AI Platform: Data and AI + Technology Foundation)
- **Solution Advisor — Cloud ERP**
- **Solution Advisor — CX** (Customer Experience)
- **Solution Advisor — Supply Chain**
- **Solution Advisor — Finance & Spend**
- **Solution Advisor — HCM** (Human Capital Management)
- **Solution Advisor — BTM** (Business Transformation Management)
- **Solution Advisor Expert** — deeper specialization within a team (e.g. Solution Advisor Expert — BDC, Solution Advisor Expert — AI)
- **Digital Solution Advisor Presales Specialist**
- **Senior Presales** (used in some regions)

### Sales
- **Account Executive** (Associate / Expert)
- **Business Development Manager**
- **Partner Account Manager**

### Architecture & Technical Consulting
- **Technology Consultant** / **Technology Senior Consultant**
- **Solution Architect** (used in services/delivery)
- **Enterprise Architect** (used in customer-facing strategic engagements)

### Delivery & Implementation
- **Technology Consultant** (delivery-focused)
- **Services Engagement Manager**
- **Implementation Consultant**

### Customer Success & Adoption
- **Customer Success Partner (CSP)**
- **Adoption Specialist**

### Development
- **Developer** / **Senior Developer**
- **IT Technology Services Expert / Specialist / Senior Specialist**

### Partner and Customer Roles
Partner and customer role titles are not part of SAP internal taxonomy. See `references/roles/roles-index.md` for the full mapping:
- **Role 9:** Partner Consultant / Partner Sales Engineer
- **Role 12:** Partner Delivery / Implementation Consultant (PARTNER mode)
- **Roles 13–17:** Customer Business Users (Finance, HR, Operations, IT, Data/General)
